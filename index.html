FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what clients ask us most.

Which services does Accuratis Advisors offer?

We handle accounting and bookkeeping, GST registration and returns, income tax (ITR) filing and planning, TDS, PF/ESI compliance, MSME/Udyam registration and general financial advisory. See the Services section above for the full list.

Do I need a GST registration for my business?

It depends on your business type, turnover and state, and the rules can change. GST registration is generally required once turnover crosses the government's threshold, and is mandatory in certain cases such as some inter-state supplies or selling through e-commerce platforms. Book a free consultation and we will check your exact situation.

Which documents are needed for income tax return filing?

Usually your PAN and Aadhaar, Form 16 or other income proofs, bank statements, Form 26AS/AIS, investment and deduction proofs (such as 80C, health insurance, home loan interest) and capital gains statements if any. Businesses also need their books, profit and loss account and balance sheet.

Can you work with me online if I am in another city?

Yes. Most of our work can be handled remotely over phone, WhatsApp and email, so you can share documents and get your filings done without visiting an office.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the scope of work, the volume of transactions and how often you need the service. We offer a free consultation, after which we share a clear quote so there are no surprises.

What happens if I miss a filing deadline?

Depending on the return, a late fee, interest or penalty may apply, and some benefits can be lost. The sooner you act the better, so contact us and we will help you file the pending returns and get back on track.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Yes. We treat all client information as confidential and use it only for the filings and advice you have asked for.

How do I get started?

Fill in the contact form below or message us on WhatsApp. We will understand your requirements in a free consultation and suggest the right plan for you.
